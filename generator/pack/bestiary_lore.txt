[Grunt]
Unggoy. The Covenant's laborers and cannon fodder: short, methane-breathing, and brave only in numbers. A Grunt fights while there is an officer to fear; kill its Elite and the squad is liable to scatter, shrieking. Left alone with a plasma grenade and nothing to lose, it may run at you holding one in each hand.

[GruntUltra]
An Unggoy veteran in white armor, survivor of more campaigns than its kin can count. Ultras stand their ground longer, take more punishment and lead lesser Grunts when no Elite is near.

[GruntHeavy]
A Grunt trusted with a fuel rod cannon and armor to match. Heavies hang back and lob green bolts that arc over cover; close the distance quickly or keep moving.

[GruntSpecOps]
Unggoy special operations: black armor, a better weapon and the discipline the rank and file lack. They follow the Spec Ops Elites onto the hard objectives and are far slower to break.

[Jackal]
Kig-Yar. Raiders and mercenaries with the keenest eyes in the Covenant, fighting behind a wrist-mounted energy shield. The shield is overcharged plasma's natural prey; otherwise, wait for the gap when it fires and shoot past the edge.

[JackalMajor]
A Kig-Yar of standing, its shield colored by rank. Majors are steadier under fire and carry better arms than the pistol-toting Minors.

[H2Jackal]
Kig-Yar as the Covenant fielded them in the war's later years: rifles and carbines instead of pistols, and marksmen who prefer to fight from far away. Expect the long-range ones to keep their distance and their heads down.

[JackalSniper]
A Kig-Yar marksman with a beam rifle. You will rarely see one before its shot; the pink tracer of the first miss is the warning.

[JackalZealot]
A Kig-Yar fanatic in gold, armed with a Brute Spiker. Rare, aggressive and unwilling to retreat.

[Elite]
Sangheili. The Covenant's warrior caste and its field officers: tall, fast, and protected by a recharging energy shield. They command the lesser races, roll out of grenade blasts, and fall back to let their shields return before pressing again. Their armor color tells their rank.

[EliteMinor]
The blue-armored Minor Domo, the lowest Elite rank and still the most dangerous soldier in most Covenant squads.

[EliteMajor]
A red-armored Major. Stronger shields than a Minor, better aim, and a commander's habit of directing the Grunts around it.

[EliteHeavy]
A green-armored Elite trusted with heavier weapons, the fuel rod among them. Heavies sit between Majors and Commanders in rank.

[EliteCommander]
A gold-armored Commander, veteran of many campaigns. Commanders lead from the front and favor the energy sword.

[EliteSpecial]
Sangheili special operations, in black armor with active camouflage. They strike from concealment and are seldom seen until they choose to be.

[EliteSpecops]
Spec Ops Elites: picked warriors with stronger shields and better weapons, assigned to the operations that matter. They work alongside the Spec Ops Grunts.

[StealthElite]
An Elite wearing an active camouflage harness. Watch for the shimmer as it moves, and for the moment it becomes visible to fire.

[EliteHonorGuard]
The High Council's ceremonial guard, chosen from the Sangheili's finest and armored in crimson and gold. They guard the Prophets themselves, and the sword is their weapon of choice.

[EliteRanger]
Sangheili trained to fight in vacuum and on hostile ground, sealed in an EVA suit and carried by a jump pack. Rangers take the high ground in a fight and drift down onto you from above.

[EliteRifle]
Sangheili armed with long Covenant carbines and pulse carbines, fighting at a distance most Elites would scorn.

[EliteZealot]
A Zealot: a Sangheili of the Covenant's most devout order, sent where the faith's interests are greatest. Expect overwhelming shields and an energy sword.

[Hunter]
Mgalekgolo. Not one creature but a colony of orange worms living as one armored giant, always deployed as a bonded pair. Their front is almost impervious; aim for the exposed orange flesh at the neck and back, and do not stand still when one lowers its shield to charge. Kill one and its brother will go berserk.

[HunterRed]
A Mgalekgolo whose arm cannon has been replaced by a flamethrower. Do not let it get close.

[HunterWhite]
A Mgalekgolo armed with a plasma caster, lobbing bolts over walls and into cover.

[Brute]
Jiralhanae. Huge, strong and quick to anger, the Brutes rose to replace the Elites as the Covenant's enforcers. They wear armor that can be knocked from their heads, and when their pack dies around them they discard their guns and charge on all fours.

[BruteStalker]
A Brute hunter wearing an active camouflage harness, stalking prey with a Spiker.

[BruteHonorGuard]
Brutes in red ceremonial armor, who took the Honor Guard's duties from the Sangheili.

[BruteChieftain]
The leader of a Brute pack, with a gravity hammer that shakes the ground at every blow. It leaps at its targets from a distance.

[BruteCaptain]
A Brute officer whose hologram banner marks it as a pack leader.

[Drone]
Yanme'e. Insectoid swarms with plasma pistols and needlers, darting through the air, clinging to walls and diving in from above. Individually fragile, dangerous in numbers.

[Engineer]
Huragok. Living machines of the Forerunners, enslaved by the Covenant to repair and maintain their technology. An Engineer will shield its allies; in this war, it may also be wired to explode.

[Drinol]
A massive beast from the Covenant's menageries, set loose on the battlefield.

[SlugMan]
A crude, enormous creature of the Covenant's fringes. Slow but hard to kill.

[BlindWolf]
A blind predator that hunts by sound and smell and spits acid.

[ThornBeast]
A spined beast that fires quills from its back.

[FloodInfection]
The Flood's infection form: a small, balloon-like parasite that moves in swarms, latches onto a host and rewrites it from within. Alone it dies to a blow; in hundreds it overwhelms. It will also seek out the dead.

[FloodCarrier]
A bloated Flood form that carries infection forms inside it and bursts when it dies or reaches its prey. Kill it at range.

[FloodGrunt]
An Unggoy taken by the Flood, bloated around a sack of infection forms (from the XAM Campaign Overhaul). Small and fast, it rushes in to claw at you, and bursts when it dies, letting out the infection forms it carried. Kill it before it reaches you.

[FloodODST]
An Orbital Drop Shock Trooper taken by the Flood (from Ruby's Rebalance), still in its black armour. Tougher than the other human forms, and it still knows how to use the rifle, shotgun or sniper rifle it died with.

[FloodMarine]
A Marine taken by the Flood in fatigues and no armour (from Ruby's Rebalance), its own head lolling on its shoulder while the Flood's grows from its chest.

[FloodArmored]
An armoured Marine taken by the Flood (from Ruby's Rebalance), its plating cracked open by the growth beneath. It fights with whatever it was carrying, rocket launchers and sniper rifles included.

[FloodEliteArmored]
A Sangheili host taken with its armour and its energy shield still working (from Ruby's Rebalance). Minors keep a weaker shield, Majors a stronger one; break it before its limbs will come off.

[FloodEliteCommander]
A gold-armoured Sangheili Commander taken by the Flood (from Ruby's Rebalance), still carrying its energy sword and a powerful shield. It charges with the blade as it did in life.

[FloodEliteUltra]
A white-armoured Ultra taken by the Flood (from Ruby's Rebalance), shielded and armed with a fuel rod cannon.

[FloodHuman]
A human host taken by the Flood: a former Marine, still wearing its armor, still able to fire the weapon in its grip. It is fast, savage, and rarely stays down.

[FloodElite]
A Sangheili host taken by the Flood. Its energy shield still works, and it hits harder than any human form.

[Sentinel]
Forerunner machines left behind to guard their installations, firing a continuous beam. Some carry shields. Once they ignored humanity; now they defend the rings against the Flood and anyone else in the way.

[Marine]
UNSC Marines: the soldiers who stood against the Covenant on every front. They follow a Spartan, take orders, pick up better weapons and carry on fighting while there is breath in them.

[MarineArmored]
Marines in heavier armor, given heavier weapons and harder jobs.

[MarineODST]
Orbital Drop Shock Troopers. They arrive feet first from orbit in drop pods, fight in sealed armor and have a reputation to maintain. Their black armor and dark visors are recognized across the Covenant's front lines.

[Hellbringer]
An ODST flamethrower specialist. Hellbringers clear the Flood out of corridors no one else will enter.

[Pilot]
A UNSC pilot in flight helmet and visor, armed for a fight on the ground.

[RadioOperator]
A Marine radioman. When the fight turns bad, the radio brings reinforcements.

[Johnson]
Sergeant Avery Johnson: veteran, inspiration, and the last man to tell you he is in trouble.

[Stacker]
Sergeant Stacker: a steady hand, never far from his men or his rifle.
