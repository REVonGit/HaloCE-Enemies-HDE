[Grunt]
Unggoy. The Covenant's laborers and cannon fodder: short, methane-breathing, and brave only in numbers. A Grunt fights while there is an officer to fear; kill its Elite and the squad is liable to scatter, shrieking. Left alone with a plasma grenade and nothing to lose, it may run at you holding one in each hand.

[GruntUltra]
An Unggoy veteran in white armor, survivor of more campaigns than its kin can count. Ultras stand their ground longer, take more punishment and lead lesser Grunts when no Elite is near.

[GruntHeavy]
A Grunt trusted with a fuel rod cannon and armor to match. Heavies hang back and lob green bolts that arc over cover; close the distance quickly or keep moving.

[GruntSpecOps]
Unggoy special operations: black armor, a better weapon and the discipline the rank and file lack. They follow the Spec Ops Elites onto the hard objectives and are far slower to break.

[Jackal]
Kig-Yar. Raiders and mercenaries with the keenest eyes in the Covenant, fighting behind a wrist-mounted energy shield. The shield is overcharged plasma's natural prey; otherwise, wait for the gap when it fires and shoot past the edge.

[JackalMajor]
A Kig-Yar of standing, its shield colored by rank. Majors are steadier under fire and carry better arms than the pistol-toting Minors.

[H2Jackal]
Kig-Yar as the Covenant fielded them in the war's later years: rifles and carbines instead of pistols, and marksmen who prefer to fight from far away. Expect the long-range ones to keep their distance and their heads down.

[JackalSniper]
A Kig-Yar marksman with a beam rifle, in a sealed sniper helmet whose lenses glow in the dark. You will rarely see one before its shot; the pink tracer of the first miss, or those two points of light on the high ground, are the warning.

[JackalZealot]
A Kig-Yar fanatic in gold, armed with a Brute Spiker. Rare, aggressive and unwilling to retreat.

[JackalMajorHelmet]
A Kig-Yar major in the old plated helmet of the first war's pirate captains, carrying a plasma rifle behind a pink point-defence shield. Tougher than the bare-headed ranks; aim for the gun hand when it peeks round the shield's edge.

[JackalMarksman]
A Kig-Yar marksman in the sniper's lensed helmet, trading the beam rifle for a carbine and a faster rate of fire. It keeps its distance and leaves the shield behind.

[Elite]
Sangheili. The Covenant's warrior caste and its field officers: tall, fast, and protected by a recharging energy shield. They command the lesser races, roll out of grenade blasts, and fall back to let their shields return before pressing again. Their armor color tells their rank.

[EliteMinor]
The blue-armored Minor Domo, the lowest Elite rank and still the most dangerous soldier in most Covenant squads.

[EliteMajor]
A red-armored Major. Stronger shields than a Minor, better aim, and a commander's habit of directing the Grunts around it.

[EliteHeavy]
A green-armored Elite trusted with heavier weapons, the fuel rod among them. Heavies sit between Majors and Commanders in rank.

[EliteCommander]
A gold-armored Commander, veteran of many campaigns. Commanders lead from the front and favor the energy sword.

[EliteSpecial]
Sangheili special operations, in black armor with active camouflage. They strike from concealment and are seldom seen until they choose to be.

[EliteSpecops]
Spec Ops Elites: picked warriors with stronger shields and better weapons, assigned to the operations that matter. They work alongside the Spec Ops Grunts.

[StealthElite]
An Elite wearing an active camouflage harness. Watch for the shimmer as it moves, and for the moment it becomes visible to fire.

[EliteHonorGuard]
The High Council's ceremonial guard, chosen from the Sangheili's finest and armored in crimson and gold. They guard the Prophets themselves, and the sword is their weapon of choice.

[EliteRanger]
Sangheili trained to fight in vacuum and on hostile ground, sealed in an EVA suit and carried by a jump pack. Rangers take the high ground in a fight and drift down onto you from above.

[EliteRifle]
Sangheili armed with long Covenant carbines and pulse carbines, fighting at a distance most Elites would scorn.

[EliteZealot]
A Zealot: a Sangheili of the Covenant's most devout order, sent where the faith's interests are greatest. Expect overwhelming shields and an energy sword.

[Hunter]
Mgalekgolo. Not one creature but a colony of orange worms living as one armored giant, always deployed as a bonded pair. Their front is almost impervious; aim for the exposed orange flesh at the neck and back, and do not stand still when one lowers its shield to charge. Kill one and its brother will go berserk.

[HunterMajor]
A veteran Mgalekgolo in red armor, its arm cannon replaced by a flamethrower. It is harder to kill than the common Hunter, and it wants to get close. Do not let it.

[HunterUltra]
The most seasoned of the Mgalekgolo, armored in white and the toughest of its kind. Its arm cannon is a plasma caster, lobbing bolts over walls and into cover.

[Brute]
Jiralhanae. Huge, strong and quick to anger, the Brutes rose to replace the Elites as the Covenant's enforcers. They wear armor that can be knocked from their heads, and when their pack dies around them they discard their guns and charge on all fours.

[BruteStalker]
A Brute hunter wearing an active camouflage harness, stalking prey with a Spiker.

[BruteHonorGuard]
Brutes in red ceremonial armor, who took the Honor Guard's duties from the Sangheili.

[BruteChieftain]
The leader of a Brute pack, with a gravity hammer that shakes the ground at every blow. It leaps at its targets from a distance.

[BruteCaptain]
A Brute officer whose hologram banner marks it as a pack leader.

[Drone]
Yanme'e. Insectoid swarms with plasma pistols and needlers, darting through the air, clinging to walls and diving in from above. Individually fragile, dangerous in numbers.

[DroneMajor]
A seasoned Yanme'e, its carapace plates marked in blue. Tougher than the swarm around it.

[DroneUltra]
A veteran of the swarm in white-plated chitin, able to take far more punishment than its hive-mates.

[DroneCaptain]
A red-plated swarm leader. Captains fly under an energy shield and often carry plasma rifles.

[DroneCaptainMajor]
The swarm's senior officer, plated in gold, with a stronger shield than a Captain's.

[Engineer]
Huragok. Living machines of the Forerunners, enslaved by the Covenant to repair and maintain their technology. An Engineer will shield its allies; in this war, it may also be wired to explode.

[Draeth]
A warrior race of the Covenant, kin to the Sangheili in build and bearing, in an older style of armour. UNSC troops who met them called them Reavers. They fight like Elites with the Covenant's guns: plasma rifles and needlers, and fuel rod cannons in the hands of Majors. Only their high ranks wear energy shields; Minors and Majors fight unshielded. Their color tells their rank: blue Minors, red Majors, white Commanders, gold Zealots.

[DraethCommander]
A Draeth commander in white. Commanders carry the Covenant's heavier arms: a beam rifle for the long shot, a Plasma Caster that lobs clusters of plasma over cover, or a fuel rod cannon. Many go into battle under active camouflage, trading their shield for the cloak; watch for the shimmer.

[DraethZealot]
A Draeth zealot in gold, above the commanders: the most devout of their warriors, tougher than any other Draeth and behind the strongest shield of their kind, armed with the commanders' heavy weapons.

[Artybug]
Karo'et-pattern Siege Crawler. UNSC designation: Beetle. A Covenant war beast, a purple-shelled walker spiked with red armour plates, with a plasma cannon at its front that fires a spray of bolts like a shotgun. It takes a great deal of punishment, and it will rush anything that gets close.

[Geist]
A small, sleek Covenant attack drone that hunts in packs. Each one carries a plasma rifle under its hull and dives in to strafe its target before darting away. A single Geist dies easily; a swarm does not.

[Drinol]
A massive beast from the Covenant's menageries, set loose on the battlefield.

[SlugMan]
A crude, enormous creature of the Covenant's fringes. Slow but hard to kill.

[BlindWolf]
A blind predator that hunts by sound and smell and spits acid.

[ThornBeast]
A spined beast that fires quills from its back.

[FloodInfection]
The Flood's infection form: a small, balloon-like parasite that moves in swarms, latches onto a host and rewrites it from within. Alone it dies to a blow; in hundreds it overwhelms. It will also seek out the dead.

[FloodCarrier]
A bloated Flood form that carries infection forms inside it and bursts when it dies or reaches its prey. Kill it at range.

[FloodGrunt]
An Unggoy taken by the Flood, bloated around a sack of infection forms. Small and fast, it rushes in to claw at you, and bursts when it dies, letting out the infection forms it carried. Kill it before it reaches you.

[FloodGruntPlasmaPistol]
An Unggoy taken by the Flood that kept its weapon. Small and quick, it fires the plasma pistol or needler it died holding, leaps in to claw at whatever comes close, and like the other combat forms it may rise again unless it is torn apart.

[FloodGruntNeedler]
An Unggoy taken by the Flood that kept its weapon. Small and quick, it fires the plasma pistol or needler it died holding, leaps in to claw at whatever comes close, and like the other combat forms it may rise again unless it is torn apart.

[FloodJuggernaut]
A hulking tank form grown from the biomass of many hosts, nearly twice a Hunter's height, with a heavy carapace and long, scything arms. It carries no weapon and needs none: it charges, leaps across the room onto its prey and smashes whatever it reaches. It shrugs off small-arms fire; bring heavy weapons, keep moving and never let it close the distance.

[FloodcombatBrute]
A Jiralhanae taken by the Flood. Its left arm has become a long, jointed claw, tendrils sprout where its head once sat, and the head itself hangs slumped on its shoulder, still wearing its rank's helmet. It keeps a Brute's bulk and strength, fires whatever weapon it held when it fell, and leaps in to tear at you with the claw. Like every combat form, it may rise again unless it is torn apart.

[FloodODST]
An Orbital Drop Shock Trooper taken by the Flood, still in its black armour. Tougher than the other human forms, and it still knows how to use the rifle, shotgun or sniper rifle it died with.

[FloodMarine]
A Marine taken by the Flood in fatigues and no armour, its own head lolling on its shoulder while the Flood's grows from its chest.

[FloodArmored]
An armoured Marine taken by the Flood, its plating cracked open by the growth beneath. It fights with whatever it was carrying, rocket launchers and sniper rifles included.

[FloodEliteArmored]
A Sangheili host taken with its armour and its energy shield still working. Minors keep a weaker shield, Majors a stronger one; break it before its limbs will come off.

[FloodEliteCommander]
A gold-armoured Sangheili Commander taken by the Flood, still carrying its energy sword and a powerful shield. It charges with the blade as it did in life.

[FloodEliteUltra]
A white-armoured Ultra taken by the Flood, shielded and armed with a fuel rod cannon.

[FloodHuman]
A human host taken by the Flood: a former Marine, still wearing its armor, still able to fire the weapon in its grip. It is fast, savage, and rarely stays down.

[FloodElite]
A Sangheili host taken by the Flood. Its energy shield still works, and it hits harder than any human form.

[Sentinel]
Forerunner machines left behind to guard their installations, firing a continuous beam. Some carry shields. They know humans for what they are: the Forerunners' descendants, heirs to everything the machines were built to keep. So the Sentinels stand with humanity, against the Flood and the Covenant alike.

[SentinelEliminator]
A Sentinel built for the purge's hardest work: a shield bubble around its hull and a beam that burns far hotter than the common Sentinel's. When one joins the fight, whatever it is burning does not last long.

[SentinelEnforcer]
A heavy Forerunner war machine that floats on hover jets. Two energy shields across its front stop anything fired from ahead; it answers with long strings of needle bolts from both arms, salvoes of homing hard-light rockets, and its claws for anything that gets close. Its back and sides are bare, so it keeps its shields turned toward the fight.

[SentinelConstructor]
A little Forerunner builder drone, made to weld and mend the installation, not to fight. Sent against intruders it does what it knows: it darts in out of nowhere, rams them, and burns at them with its cutting beam. It cannot take much punishment.

[SentinelKnight]
A Forerunner war machine of hard light, built around a burning core. A Knight fights with the weapon that forms its right forearm and the blade on its left, and when its shield fails it sinks into the ground in a flash of light and rises again somewhere else, often right beside its foe.

[SentinelKnightMinor]
The common Knight, plated in grey, its core burning a cold colour.

[SentinelKnightMajor]
A seasoned Knight with a stronger shield and more armour.

[SentinelKnightCommander]
A gold-plated Knight commander with a warm-burning core: the toughest of its kind, with a heavy shield.

[SentinelSoldier]
The heaviest of the new Forerunner guardians: a broad, armoured hull with a strong shield and three beams: its own, and one from a gun on either side of the hull, each firing on its own. It takes a sustained effort to bring one down, and it soaks up fire the whole while.

[SentinelDragoon]
A long, T-shaped Forerunner gunship with a heavy shield. It carries the Enforcer's needle cannon, raking an area with strings of hard-light bolts from range, and a heavy beam gun under its nose that burns in long bursts at the same time.

[SentinelGold]
A Sentinel cast in gold, burning a cold blue beam instead of the usual one. Majors are tougher, and the Shielded models carry an energy shield that has to be stripped first.

[SentinelOnyx]
A dark Forerunner drone of a different make, compact and heavily shielded, with a blue beam. It is slow to kill and quick to punish any foe that stands in the open.

[SentinelWarden]
A well-shielded guardian of the installation, built to hold ground. Its beam is the common Sentinel's; its staying power is not.

[SentinelProtector]
A light escort drone with the Enforcer's needle cannon. On its own it is no great threat, but its hard-light bolts add up fast when it flies with others.

[SentinelInterceptor]
A slim, fast attack drone that fires needle bolts as it swoops. It cannot take much punishment, so it relies on speed, striking before its target can turn.

[SentinelLancer]
A Forerunner burner. It flies in close and sprays fire across whatever it finds, which makes it deadly in tight corridors and easy prey at range.

[SentinelMarauder]
A cross-winged Forerunner raider with a standard beam. It is light and quick, meant to harass rather than hold a line.

[SentinelCrawler]
A Forerunner machine that walks instead of flying, scuttling across the floor on its legs. It keeps a beam for range and lunges at anything that gets close.

[SentinelArtillery]
A Forerunner bombardment drone. It hangs back out of reach and lobs heavy shells of hard light over cover, bursting where they fall; a foe who stays in one place behind a wall is still not safe from it.

[SentinelBrute]
A flying fortress of a Sentinel, many times the size of the common kind and armoured to match. It burns its targets with a heavy beam and lobs artillery shells between bursts. Bringing one down takes everything you have.

[SentinelCanoneer]
A heavy gun platform among the Sentinels, built around a beam far stronger than the common one. It burns through shields at mid range; break line of sight before it settles on you.

[SentinelCompiler]
A Sentinel that fires a spread of hard-light bolts at once, scattering them across a corridor like a shotgun blast. Deadly up close, much less so at range.

[SentinelOmega]
A Forerunner hunter of an older pattern, sharp-hulled and tougher than it looks, with a heavy beam.

[SentinelScout]
A small, fast Sentinel that strings hard-light needle bolts at its targets. It is fragile, but rarely alone, and it marks intruders for the heavier machines behind it.

[Marine]
UNSC Marines: the soldiers who stood against the Covenant on every front. They follow a Spartan, take orders, pick up better weapons and carry on fighting while there is breath in them.

[MarineArmored]
Marines in heavier armor, given heavier weapons and harder jobs.

[MarineODST]
Orbital Drop Shock Troopers. They arrive feet first from orbit in drop pods, fight in sealed armor and have a reputation to maintain. Their black armor and dark visors are recognized across the Covenant's front lines.

[MarineMedic]
A Navy corpsman serving with the Marines: a rifleman like the rest until someone goes down, then the one who runs to him under fire and patches him up. Call for a medic and the nearest corpsman comes.

[MarineFieldMedic]
A UNSC field medic in the white of the medical corps, under a red-cross cap. Medics carry a sidearm and use it, but their work is keeping the squad alive: call for a medic and one will come running to whoever needs patching up.

[MarineRifleman]
A UNSC Marine rifleman in brown composite armour, his face hidden behind a balaclava and an amber visor, his hands in black combat gloves. He fights like the rest of the squad, with an assault rifle, battle rifle or shotgun, and picks up whatever the fight leaves lying around.

[Hellbringer]
An ODST flamethrower specialist. Hellbringers clear the Flood out of corridors no one else will enter.

[Pilot]
A UNSC pilot in flight helmet and visor, armed for a fight on the ground.

[RadioOperator]
A Marine radioman. When the fight turns bad, the radio brings reinforcements.

[Johnson]
Sergeant Avery Johnson: veteran, inspiration, and the last man to tell you he is in trouble.

[Stacker]
Sergeant Stacker: a steady hand, never far from his men or his rifle.
