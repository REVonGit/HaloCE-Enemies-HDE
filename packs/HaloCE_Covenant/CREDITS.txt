Halo CE enemy pack: credits
===========================

This is the main pack (the Core, the enemy API, the Covenant, and the Digsite / SPV3 / Halo 2 enemies);
the Flood, Sentinels and Marines packs load with it.

Digsite content: NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE outside MCC mod projects.
- Drinol and Slug Man models, animations, textures, AI stats, the Slug Man's dialogue and his
  Particle Beam Rifle (99_mac), and the prototype guns (the Sidekick, MA37 and DMR, and Sergeant
  Johnson's Stanchion, from the Macworld and E3 1999-2000 builds) come from the Halo CE Digsite release
  (github.com/digsite/h1). Its README states the content is not open source and is licensed only for
  use in Halo: The Master Chief Collection mod projects.
- The Elites' Covenant carbine is CMT's (cmt\weapons\_shared\carbine): model and textures from the
  CMT tags; the purple carapace tint is baked from its shader's reflection colours. The Pulse Carbine
  Elites carry a blue re-tint of it.

Other creatures and kits:
- The Blind Wolf was created by SOI_7 (model, animations, texture, stats); its dialogue comes from the
  HaloDoomEnemies sound set. The Thorn Beast is omargabagu's, its sounds Ruby of Blue's. Both are
  extracted from SPV3's a30 map.
- The Hunter's, Elites', Grunts' and Jackals' added and reworked animations are SOI7's, from Halo:
  Brutality Evolved by RoxanneGoat (MCC Steam Workshop 3014096841); the Brutes' Halo 3-style animations
  come from the same mod's Brutes, credited there to "Elite".
- The Hunter's leaping spin attack is from Cursed Halo Again by InfernoPlus (MCC Steam Workshop
  2962107814).
- The Slug Man armour maps (slugman multi) are Sgt Stacker's (Nullen), from the Assorted Character Tags
  Pack (Nexus Mods, Halo: MCC mod 2664).
- The Engineer comes from Halo CE Ruby's Rebalance (model, texture, animations, dialogue), extracted
  from SPV3's b30 map.
- Elite rifle stance: Halo CE Elite rifle animations (CE rig) from the supplied elite.model_animations,
  with a two-bone IK touch-up keeping the support hand on the carbine.
- Brute armour kit: the h2_brute armour set (brute.blend with its bitmaps): the "King Hit" helmets,
  chest plates, shoulder pads, bracers, belts and leg armour, the Halo 3-style Minor / Major / Captain
  armour and the jump-pack and chieftain suits, all rigged to the Halo 2 Brute skeleton.
- Additional Brute and Elite content by Shigure. The Ultra Zealot is Shigure's Zealot Elite
  (Zealot helmet, ornament, torso and arm plates, the diamond arm shield) on the Halo CE Elite.
- The Elite Vanguard animations (SPV3's shield Elite, used for the Ultra Zealot's sword stance) are by
  Masterz1337 and Ruby of Blue.
- Drone ranks (Major, Ultra, Captain, Captain Major): Ruby of Blue's "H2 Drone Rank Tags".
- Halo 2 Jackal armour and helmets: IanLacksMods' "Halo 2 - Characters+ Tag Pack".
- The Halo 3 Spiker: a community Halo CE port of Halo 3's.

The rest of the packs:
- Gore stumps and gore textures and the bursting Flood Grunt's AI and sounds: Tropical Thunder 98,
  from the XAM Campaign Overhaul (SPV3's c40 map).
- The Flood Grunt's model, skins and animations and the armed Flood Grunts: Sabopool's Halo CE Overhaul 2.0
  (MCC Steam Workshop 3735328357).
- The Marine riflemen: Shigure's Marine, from Sabopool's Halo CE Overhaul 2.0 (its gloves that mod's ODST's).
- Halo 2 Elite shoulder stumps: Hunterunt's Restored Gore features Halo 2.
- Ruby's Rebalance's Flood hosts (ODST, Marine, Armored Marine, shielded Elites, Elite Ultra and
  Commander): mynameislol, from Ruby's Rebalance (its c40 map).
- The Flood Juggernaut and the double barrel (SOI's 8-gauge shotgun): Halo: SOI's Singleplayer
  Adventure V3 by SOI (MCC Steam Workshop 2906165512).
- Mercury Rising by aLTis (MCC Steam Workshop 3021510694), with its own credits: the Artillery, Brute, Canoneer,
  Compiler, Omega and Scout Sentinels, the Artybug and the Marine variants (the medic among them): unevenelefant5.
  The Geist: Arteen (CMT). The Draeth is the Digsite release's 1999 Elite (99_mac). The Draeth's and the Geist's stats and
  loadouts are the mod's.
- The Reavers' (Draeth) voice and footsteps: Black Mesa's Alien Grunt (Crowbar Collective).
- Sentinel Knight: blackandfan's Halo 2 tag pack (H2EK-Tags); its voice: REV.
- Flood Brute: blackandfan's Halo 2 tag pack (H2EK-Tags); its fronds: SOI's Flood Juggernaut.
- Galap's Sentinels (Soldier, Dragoon, Gold, Onyx, Warden, Protector, Lancer, Interceptor, Marauder,
  Crawler): Galap; the Crawler's biped: Lone Warrior.
- Forerunner arm guns: HDE_promethian_weapons.blend (HaloDoom Evolved).
- Marine kit: Elefant (unevenelefant5). ODSTs: Spiral. Fire Team Raven ODSTs: Connor Dawn. Marine flamethrower
  animations: The Fluffers (with thanks to Spiral).
- Voice lines: Lewisk3/HaloDoomEnemies (through the REVonGit/HaloDoomEnemies-Proto fork).
- HaloDoom Evolved team: HDE, the enemy API base, and the sounds, models and effects the packs use
  from it (the Suppressor's fire sounds among them).

Halo assets are Microsoft / 343 Industries / Bungie property, used under the Game Content Usage Rules
(non-commercial).
