Digsite add-on for the Halo CE enemy pack
=========================================

NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE.

- Drinol and Slug Man models, animations, textures, AI stats, the Slug Man's dialogue and his
  Particle Beam Rifle (99_mac) come from the Halo CE Digsite release (github.com/digsite/h1).
  Its README states the content is not open source and is licensed only for use in
  Halo: The Master Chief Collection mod projects.
- The Elites' Covenant carbine is CMT's (cmt\weapons\_shared\carbine): model and textures from the
  CMT tags; the purple carapace tint is baked from its shader's reflection colours.
- The Blind Wolf and the Thorn Beast (models, animations, textures, stats) are extracted from SPV3's
  a30 map (CMT). The Blind Wolf's dialogue comes from the HaloDoomEnemies sound set; the Thorn Beast's
  sounds are its own, from the same map.
- The Pulse Carbine Elites carry a blue re-tint of the same CMT carbine.
- Elite rifle stance: Halo CE Elite rifle animations (CE rig) from the supplied elite.model_animations,
  with a two-bone IK touch-up keeping the support hand on the carbine.
- Brute armour kit: the h2_brute armour set (brute.blend with its bitmaps): the "King Hit" helmets,
  chest plates, shoulder pads, bracers, belts and leg armour, the Halo 3-style Minor / Major / Captain
  armour and the jump-pack and chieftain suits, all rigged to the Halo 2 Brute skeleton. Read straight
  from the .blend; Halo 3-style pieces are tinted per rank through their change-colour mask.

Halo assets are Microsoft / Bungie property.
